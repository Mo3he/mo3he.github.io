Axis camera project hub

Independent apps I build for Axis devices. Open a project to see what it does, explore a sample preview, or download it for your camera.

Remote accessVideo & recordingsAutomationMonitoring

Video & recordings

  • MediaMTXShare a live camera stream and browse recordings on a timeline.
  • Recording ExportFind an incident on the SD card, trim the footage and export clips in bulk.
  • Whisper SubtitlesTurn microphone audio into captions without sending it to the cloud.
  • go2rtcBring camera video into a browser or an existing Home Assistant streaming setup.

Automation & industrial integrations

  • Event EngineBuild rules on the camera: when an event matches, record, notify or trigger another system.
  • BACnet ServerLet a building management system read camera events and sensor values.
  • Sparkplug Edge NodePublish camera and sensor readings to an industrial monitoring system over MQTT.

Monitoring & telemetry

  • Camera MetricsSee how the device is doing now and check its temperature, storage and CPU history.
  • InfluxDB Data CollectorSend device and sensor readings to your own InfluxDB v2 database.

Remote access

  • TailscaleJoin the camera to your tailnet for access from your own devices.
  • NetBirdEnroll a camera in NetBird Cloud or a self-hosted network.
  • ZeroTierReach selected camera services from authorized members of your ZeroTier network.
  • WireGuard ClientConnect the camera to a WireGuard server you already run.
  • WireGuard ServerConnect your phone or laptop to the camera instead, with optional access to its local network.
  • OpenVPNImport an OpenVPN profile to connect the camera to your VPN gateway.
  • OpenConnectConnect a camera to a GlobalProtect or Prisma Access gateway.
  • TailcatSet up a temporary troubleshooting tunnel without joining a tailnet.
