Video & recordings

Stream, record, find and caption what the camera sees.

  • MediaMTX

    Share a live camera stream and browse recordings on a timeline.

  • Recording Export

    Find an incident on the SD card, trim the footage and export clips in bulk.

  • Whisper Subtitles

    Turn microphone audio into captions without sending it to the cloud.

  • go2rtc

    Bring camera video into a browser or an existing Home Assistant streaming setup.

Automation & industrial integrations

Make the camera react to events and talk to building and factory systems.

  • Event Engine

    Build rules on the camera: when an event matches, record, notify or trigger another system.

  • BACnet Server

    Let a building management system read camera events and sensor values.

  • Sparkplug Edge Node

    Publish camera and sensor readings to an industrial monitoring system over MQTT.

Monitoring & telemetry

Keep an eye on device health and send readings to your own dashboards.

  • Camera Metrics

    See how the device is doing now and check its temperature, storage and CPU history.

  • InfluxDB Data Collector

    Send device and sensor readings to your own InfluxDB v2 database.

Remote access

Reach your camera securely from anywhere, using the VPN you already have.

  • Tailscale

    Join the camera to your tailnet for access from your own devices.

  • NetBird

    Enroll a camera in NetBird Cloud or a self-hosted network.

  • ZeroTier

    Reach selected camera services from authorized members of your ZeroTier network.

  • WireGuard Client

    Connect the camera to a WireGuard server you already run.

  • WireGuard Server

    Connect your phone or laptop to the camera instead, with optional access to its local network.

  • OpenVPN

    Import an OpenVPN profile to connect the camera to your VPN gateway.

  • OpenConnect

    Connect a camera to a GlobalProtect or Prisma Access gateway.

  • Tailcat

    Set up a temporary troubleshooting tunnel without joining a tailnet.
