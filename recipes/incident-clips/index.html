Recipe

Pull clips of an incident from SD card recordings

Find every recording around an incident, trim it to the moment that matters and export the clips in one go.

The situation

After an incident, someone has to find the right footage, cut it down and hand it over. In the camera's own interface that means downloading recordings one at a time. Recording Export lists every recording with filters, lets you trim and exports a whole selection at once without re-encoding.

What you need

  • An Axis camera with recordings on its SD card or a network share
  • A web browser (Chrome or Edge can save straight into a folder)

Steps

  1. Download the latest .eap from the Recording Export releases, install it under Apps, then open it with Apps → Recording Export → Open.
  2. Pick the storage (SD card or network share) and narrow the list: a date range, a daily time window such as 22:00 to 06:00, or the event that triggered the recording.
  3. Play a recording in the built-in player, at up to 8x, and mark a start and end so only that part is exported.
  4. Select the recordings you need, choose MP4 or MKV and a file name pattern, for example camera name, date and time.
  5. Export. Clips are processed one at a time with progress for each; failures are retried automatically.
  6. Optionally save the CSV report and a metadata file next to each clip, so you have a record of what was handed over.

Good to know

  • The original video is kept without re-encoding, in the codec the camera recorded (H.264, H.265 or AV1).
  • If your browser cannot play a codec, you can still export the clip and play it in VLC.
  • In folder mode a cancelled export leaves no half-written file, so you can run the same selection again to resume.
